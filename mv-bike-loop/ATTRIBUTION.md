## Notes on sensitive content

**Jaws Bridge.** Jumping from this bridge is a long-standing island tradition
and it has also killed people, most recently two brothers in 2022. The tour
treats both facts as true: it does not forbid the jump, and it does not
romanticize it. Each persona gives the tide and current honestly, and the
operator persona provides a concrete slack-tide reading procedure.

**The Inkwell.** The name originated as a racial slur and was reclaimed with
pride by the Black families who summered in Oak Bluffs. It is presented as
documented history with the gravity the subject deserves.

**Whaling.** The tour does not romanticize the whaling economy. It names the
crews — Wampanoag, Cape Verdean, Azorean, and Black sailors — who took the
greatest risk for the smallest share.

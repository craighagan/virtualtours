## Voice Synthesis

Narration audio was generated using **Kokoro TTS** ([kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), MIT license).

Voices used:
- Gus (oldtimer): `am_santa`
- Onyx (operator): `am_onyx`
- Nova (confidante): `af_nova`
- Jessica (fieldnaturalist): `af_jessica`

Kokoro is a free, local, open-source text-to-speech model. No cloud API, no per-character cost, no account required. Audio output is unencumbered by the model's MIT license.

## Tour Content

All narration scripts — the words each persona speaks — are original creative writing. They are informed by the research sources above but are not reproductions of any source material. Facts were verified against multiple sources where possible; unverified claims are marked as such in each tour's dossier fields.

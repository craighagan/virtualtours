## Note on the Inkwell

The Inkwell beach carries significant African American history. The narration
treats its name — originally a racial slur, later reclaimed with pride by the
Black families who summered in Oak Bluffs — as documented historical fact, drawn
from BlackPast.org and the Oak Bluffs town history. It is presented with the
gravity the subject deserves.

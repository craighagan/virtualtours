# Privacy Policy — Footnotes

Last updated: August 2026

## The short version

Footnotes doesn't collect your data. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly share it.

## Details

**Location:** Used on-device only to trigger narration when you arrive at a tour stop. Your location is never transmitted to any server, never stored beyond the current walking session, and never shared with anyone.

**No accounts:** No login, no registration, no email collection, no user profiles.

**No analytics:** No telemetry, no crash reporting, no usage tracking, no third-party SDKs. We have no idea how many people use this app or what they do with it.

**No network calls:** The app only connects to the internet when you explicitly choose to download a tour from the Browse Tours catalog. It never phones home, never syncs, never uploads.

**Local data:** Field notes, draft pins, voice memos, and downloaded tours are stored locally on your device. They only leave your phone when you tap Share and choose where to send them.

**Audio:** Narration is pre-rendered and bundled with each tour. No audio is recorded or transmitted during playback. Builder Mode's voice memo feature records only when you explicitly tap the record button, stores locally, and exports only when you choose to share.

**Children:** This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. The app itself will never collect data regardless of policy updates — the architecture doesn't support it.
